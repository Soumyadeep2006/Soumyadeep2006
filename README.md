## 🚀 Featured Project — VyaparAI

<div align="center">
<img src="https://capsule-render.vercel.app/api?type=rect&color=0:302B63,100:24243E&height=3&section=header" width="100%"/>
</div>

<table>
<tr>
<td width="60%" valign="top">

**VyaparAI** is an **AI-powered Business Operating System** built to replace the notebooks-and-spreadsheets chaos small businesses run on. One platform handles billing, inventory, customers, suppliers, and reporting — and a Gemini-powered AI layer turns that raw business data into plain-language insights business owners can actually act on.

**Core modules**
- 🧾 Smart billing & invoicing with live payment status
- 📦 Inventory tracking with low-stock alerts
- 👥 Customer & 🏢 supplier management with outstanding-payment tracking
- 📊 Sales, revenue & product-performance analytics
- 🤖 AI assistant that answers questions like *"which products need restocking?"* in natural language

**Architecture:** React + TypeScript + Vite frontend → FastAPI REST backend → MongoDB, with a Google Gemini-powered analytics layer on top. Deployed on Vercel (frontend) and Render (backend).
